# Changelog

What changed in each version of Lucy on Wally (`goatsoft/lucy`). The npm
packages keep their own: `libs/icons/CHANGELOG.md` and `libs/mcp/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Unreleased

- An App that picks no colours now starts in Lucy's own: zinc greys with a
  violet primary, as on the website. `primary = "none"` gives the old black and
  white look. `Lucy.Tokens.spectrum` holds the mascot's magenta, orchid and sky
  to use as named colours.
