# Recipes

Each recipe is a whole piece of a game, written out in full, that you can copy
and change. They use only what the guides explain, and link to them as they go.

- [Settings menu that saves](settings.md): a volume slider and a theme picker
  bound to app state, kept between sessions
- [Pause menu](pause-menu.md): opens on B or Backspace and from a button, with a
  dimmed backdrop, options on top, and gameplay keys off while it is open
- [HUD](hud.md): coins that pop when they change, a level badge and a health bar
- [Shop](shop.md): a grid of items, a confirm before buying, and a hotkey
